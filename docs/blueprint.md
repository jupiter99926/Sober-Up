# **App Name**: Road to Recovery

## Core Features:

- AI Recovery Plan Generator: Analyze user data to generate personalized recovery plans, adjusting dynamically based on progress and feedback. The LLM is used as a tool to decide which factors are most important for the user's recovery.
- User Questionnaire: Allow users to respond to a questionnaire, to help personalize the recovery plans.
- Progress Tracking: Visually track progress and milestones, such as days sober and health improvements.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: calming blue (#A0E7E5) to promote relaxation and trust.
- Secondary color: soft green (#C4E538) to represent growth and renewal.
- Accent color: warm orange (#FFB347) for positive reinforcement and highlights.
- Clean and intuitive layout with clear navigation.
- Use encouraging and supportive icons throughout the app.
- Subtle transitions and animations to provide positive feedback and engagement.

## Original User Request:
Create a full stack functional AI-powered app to help people quit drugs . Below are **ideas, features, and considerations** to make your app effective, empathetic, and user-centric:

---

### **Core Features**
1.  Users to answer the survey questions like: what type of drug the user is addicted to? (weed, alcohol, cocaine etc.), how long the user has been addicted to drugs? how may drugs the user uses per day or week?. The ai will use the data based on the drug the user is addicted to, to generate Personalized Recovery Plans, to track, detect ad prevent addiction
2. **Personalized Recovery Plans**  
   - Use AI to analyze user data (e.g., substance type, addiction severity, triggers, mental health history) to create tailored recovery roadmaps.  
   - Adjust plans dynamically based on progress, relapses, or user feedback.

3. **24/7 AI Chat Support**  
   - A conversational AI assistant trained in addiction counseling to:  
     - Provide immediate support during cravings.  
     - Offer coping strategies (e.g., mindfulness exercises, distraction techniques).  
     - Answer questions about withdrawal symptoms.  
   - Use NLP (Natural Language Processing) to detect emotional distress and respond empathetically.

4. **Craving Detection & Intervention**  
   - **Trigger Tracking**: Let users log cravings, moods, and situations in real time.  
   - **Predictive Alerts**: Use AI to predict high-risk times for cravings (e.g., based on location, time of day, or social patterns) and send proactive coping tips.  
   - **Emergency Hotline Integration**: Redirect users to crisis hotlines or local support if they’re at risk of relapse.

5. **Progress Tracking & Gamification**  
   - Visualize milestones (e.g., days sober, money saved, health improvements).  
   - Reward users with badges, streaks, or unlockable content (e.g., motivational stories).  
   - Share anonymized success stories to inspire others.

6. **Community Support**  
   - Anonymous peer-to-peer forums moderated by AI for safety.  
   - Group challenges (e.g., "30-Day Sobriety Sprint") with leaderboards.  
   - Connect users with local support groups or sponsors.

7. **Health Monitoring**  
   - Integrate with wearables (e.g., Fitbit, Apple Watch) to track physiological signs of stress (heart rate, sleep patterns).  
   - AI analyzes data to recommend interventions (e.g., "Your stress levels are high—try a 5-minute meditation").

8. **Relapse Prevention Toolkit**  
   - Interactive tools like:  
     - Distraction games during cravings.  
     - Guided meditations or breathing exercises.  
     - Emergency contact lists.  
   - Post-relapse guidance to help users restart their journey without shame.
